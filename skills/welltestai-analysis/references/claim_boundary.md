# Claim Boundary

Allowed statements:

- WellTestAI Slug Alpha provides local screening of slug/recovery response modes.
- The report identifies QC warnings, residual behavior, and posterior-style parameter intervals.
- Slug-test interpretation depends on well geometry, time-scale metadata, normalization, and early-time sampling.
- Oscillatory records can be routed to an Inertial-LDL screening branch instead of being forced into monotonic recovery.
- Oscillatory reports provide damping, frequency, and LDL response-coordinate screening when supported by the data.
- Lagging coordinates can be used as effective response-time coordinates when supported by the screening evidence.
- Broad intervals or mode ambiguity indicate limited identifiability, not software failure by itself.

Avoid these statements:

- The tool proves a unique aquifer mechanism from one slug curve.
- The alpha release replaces formal engineering judgment.
- Lagging parameters are unique physical constants without identifiability evidence.
- Oscillatory damping, frequency, or LDL tau coordinates are final aquifer hydraulic-conductivity estimates.
- The bundled Skill currently releases constant-rate pumping or constant-head-test interpretation.
- The report is a regulatory design report without independent review.

Recommended external wording:

> The analysis provides a local slug-test screening result and QC-guided interpretation. Final engineering interpretation should consider well construction, site geology, test execution, and independent hydrogeologic evidence.
